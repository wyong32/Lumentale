# LumenTale Wiki & Guides — [lumentale.org](https://lumentale.org)

**[lumentale.org](https://lumentale.org)** is a player-run reference site for **LumenTale: Memories of Trey**. It brings together route guides, an Animon dex, type charts, evolution paths, and fast wiki lookups for skills, items, recipes, and bosses — so you can plan your Talea run without juggling scattered notes mid-fight.

---

## About the Game

**LumenTale: Memories of Trey** is a creature-collecting RPG set in **Talea**, where you raise **Animon** partners, explore routes and cities, and fight in **4v4 battles** built around shared **SP** (skill points), elemental typings, and **emotional affinities**.

You choose a starter, capture wild species, evolve lines with level gates and items, cook and craft at the **Fountain**, and push through story bosses with multi-bar HP pools. The game rewards preparation: knowing type matchups, evolution branches, shop prices, and hidden mechanics often matters more than raw level grinding.

---

## What Makes LumenTale Stand Out

- **4v4 combat with shared SP** — Every move on your bench draws from one pool, so turn order, swaps, and cheap setup moves are part of team building, not an afterthought.
- **13 element types + 5 emotional affinities** — Elements handle damage matchups; affinities add another layer of bonuses when your party’s moods align in battle.
- **Branching evolutions** — Many lines split at key levels (often around 18 and 40) depending on items, stats, or story choices; picking the wrong branch early can waste rare materials.
- **Talea as a living map** — Routes, caves, restaurants, and cities each unlock new capture pools, stat meals, crafting reagents, and side content.
- **Economy and optimization loops** — Gold farming, IV-boosting restaurant meals, Fountain recipes, and evolution items all tie into mid-game progression; small systems stack quickly.
- **Boss fights that demand planning** — Multi-phase bosses, type resistances, and bar-based HP mean you often need a lookup page open before you commit levels.

## About This Site

[lumentale.org](https://lumentale.org) is maintained by the **LumenTale Fan Guide Team**. Pages are written for players who already have the game open: short intros, filterable tables, and cross-links between dex entries, wiki rows, and long-form guides.

Guides cover route walkthroughs, team building, and mechanics the game does not spell out clearly. Wiki pages are built for quick search — skills, shop items, cooking/crafting recipes, and boss stat bars — with data checked in-game where possible.

### How do SP and 4v4 battles work?

Your active party shares one **SP pool**. Every skill costs SP, so cheap moves, swaps, and bench planning matter as much as raw damage. The [Beginner](https://lumentale.org/beginner) and [Affinities](https://lumentale.org/affinities) pages explain early habits; the skills wiki lists exact costs per move.

### When should I evolve my Animon?

Many lines have **branching evolutions** with level and item gates. Evolving too early can lock a weaker form or waste a rare stone. Check **[Evolutions](https://lumentale.org/evolutions)** and the species detail page before you confirm an evolution prompt — especially for split lines like Melotica.

### How do elements and affinities differ?

**Elements** (13 types) drive standard damage matchups. **Emotional affinities** (5 types) add a separate combat layer when party mood aligns. Both appear on dex cards; the full chart is on **[Affinities](https://lumentale.org/affinities)**.

### Where are shop prices, recipes, and boss levels?

Use the wiki: **[Items](https://lumentale.org/wiki/items)**, **[Cooking](https://lumentale.org/wiki/cooking)**, **[Crafting](https://lumentale.org/wiki/crafting)**, and **[Bosses](https://lumentale.org/wiki/bosses)**. Tables support filters so you can search by name or type during a session.

### How often is the site updated?

Dex and wiki tables are updated when new game data is verified. **[Guides](https://lumentale.org/guides)** grow as new articles are published. Early Access builds can change numbers — if something looks wrong, use **[Contact Us](https://lumentale.org/legal/contact-us)** on the site.
